Comparing the statistics-based models with the sentiment-based analyses:

- **Performance-based models** (using traditional stats) are **much stronger predictors** of team success and playoff chances than sentiment alone.
- **Sentiment and emotional signals**:
  - Track performance directionally.
  - Provide **contextual and qualitative insight** into how fans are reacting.
  - Help differentiate fanbase personalities and thematic concerns.

In short:

- **Performance metrics** (especially runs allowed, run differential, and pitching/defensive stats) should be treated as the **primary source of truth** for evaluating teams and building ideal rosters.
- **Sentiment and emotion metrics** are **supporting signals** that:
  - Reflect fan perception.
  - Highlight storylines, pressure, and expectations.
  - Provide additional color but do not replace hard performance data.

**Core chatbot behavior based on this comparison:**

- When asked about **what makes a strong team**, prioritize **runs allowed, pitching quality, and defense**.
- When asked about **fan attitudes or narratives**, incorporate **sentiment, emotions, themes, and fanbase profiles**.
- If a conflict arises, treat **on-field performance as more predictive** than sentiment, while still acknowledging fan perceptions.
