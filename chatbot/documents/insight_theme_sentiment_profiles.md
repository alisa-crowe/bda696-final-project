# Insight: Sentiment Profiles Across Discussion Themes

This analysis examines the average sentiment score associated with each major discussion theme in fan discourse. Theme-level sentiment reveals how different types of conversations contribute to positive or negative fan atmosphere.

## Key Findings

### 1. Culture, Humor, and Fan Experience Drive the Most Positive Sentiment
Themes with the highest sentiment include:

- **Baseball culture & nostalgia**  
  - Highest overall sentiment.  
  - Fans respond positively to historical references, iconic moments, traditions, and storytelling.

- **Humor & memes**  
  - Strong positive sentiment.  
  - Lighthearted content reliably boosts fan morale and engagement.

- **Error (mis-tagged or auto-detected)**  
  - Often reflects casual or joke-driven content that trends positive.

- **Fan experience**  
  - Positive sentiment tied to in-stadium experiences, community, and emotional connection to the game.

These categories indicate that **positive emotional engagement is strongest in cultural, recreational, and communal contexts**, not performance-related discussions.

### 2. Performance-Focused Themes Show Moderate Sentiment
Themes centered on team outcomes or strategic discussion show **neutral-to-moderate sentiment**:

- **Team performance**
- **Offseason & future outlook**
- **Player performance**
- **League policies & fairness**

These conversations tend to be more analytical and mixed in tone—containing both praise and constructive criticism—resulting in mid-range sentiment scores.

### 3. Critical or Analytical Themes Show More Negative Sentiment
Several themes skew clearly negative:

- **Management decisions**  
  - Reflects frustration with coaching, front-office moves, or tactical choices.

- **Media & analysis**  
  - Often includes critical takes, disagreements with analysts, or negative reporting.

- **Off-topic or cross-sport content**  
  - Lowest sentiment overall.  
  - Fans generally react negatively when content is not relevant to their team or baseball.

These categories signal that **fans express disappointment, criticism, or irritation** in response to leadership decisions and analysis-heavy discourse.

## Interpretation

Theme-level sentiment highlights the **contextual drivers of fan emotion**:

- Fans are most positive when discussing **identity, culture, camaraderie, and humor**.  
- Conversations related to **on-field performance** generate balanced emotional responses.  
- Themes involving **decision-making, media framing, or irrelevant content** produce the most negativity.

The distribution also shows that **high or low team performance is not the only driver of sentiment**; the type of conversation itself heavily shapes emotional tone.

## Implications for the Chatbot

When interpreting or summarizing fan discourse:

- Emphasize that **positive fan sentiment often comes from cultural connection and humor**, not just team success.
- Recognize that **negative sentiment around management or media** does not necessarily reflect bad team performance.
- Provide nuance in explanations:
  - Example: “This team’s sentiment is low mainly because fans are frustrated with management decisions, even though the team’s performance is average.”
- Use theme-level sentiment to contextualize discussions about:
  - Fan morale  
  - Narrative framing  
  - Community dynamics  
  - Sources of optimism or frustration

These insights help the chatbot deliver **context-aware, narrative-rich explanations** grounded in authentic fan behavior patterns.
