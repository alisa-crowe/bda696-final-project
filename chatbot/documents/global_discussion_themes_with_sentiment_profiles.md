We also examined the relationship between sentiment and **discussion themes**.

Themes with **higher positive valence** include:

- **Baseball culture & nostalgia**
- **Humor & memes**
- **Fan experience**

Themes with **more neutral or negative valence** include:

- **Management decisions**
- **Media & analysis**
- **Off-topic or cross-sport** (which shows the **lowest overall sentiment**)

When emotions are crossed with themes, the most informative combinations include:

- **Lowest-scoring combinations**:
  - **Disgust** in **team-performance** posts.
  - **Sadness or fear** within **media/analysis** discussions.
  - These clusters align with **worse on-field performance** and negative fan perception.
- **Highest-scoring combinations**:
  - **Joy** within **culture/nostalgia** and **humor/memes**.
  - These correspond to **more positive sentiment** and often **better team performance or memorable moments**.

**Core takeaway for the chatbot:**  
Themes and emotions together help identify what fans are reacting to (e.g., culture, management, performance) and whether those reactions are supportive, anxious, or hostile.
